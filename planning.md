### Problem statement

This project aims to bridge gaps by creating an interactive, website that leverages the Bristol Open Data API. The website will provide real-time maps, and have practical resources tailored for users, empowering them to understand and act on air quality issues in Bristol.
The increasing levels of air pollution in Bristol pose significant health and environmental challenges for its people and decision-makers. There is a lack of accessible, user-friendly platforms that allow individuals, communities, and policymakers to easily analyze and understand this information.
